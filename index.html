What I Do

📖 Sci-Fi Author

Six-book space opera series spanning Galactic Satori and the Essence Chronicles (Septima, Alta, Amber...). Coming-of-age tales of unlikely heroes, alien essence parasites, and the fight to save Earth. Books 1–3 published; 4–6 written; Book 7 in planning.

🎵 Music Creator

30+ albums and 100+ songs across Christian metal/rock, rock, blues, jazz, and kids music — all created with AI music tools. Recent releases include Defiance Eternal, Gears of Tyranny, and Alta's Story.

🎥 Short Films & Visuals

Original short film concepts with posters, gothic video loops, and character art. Exploring AI video generation for cinematic storytelling.

♔ Tabletop & Worlds

D&D Dungeon Master, GURPS sci-fi solo campaigns (lunar colony RPG), and ongoing narrative adventures. Building AI-powered game master tools for persistent worlds.
